## 🔎 My Analytics Approach

For every project, I try to answer five important questions:

**1️⃣ What happened?**
Understand the current situation through data.

**2️⃣ Why did it happen?**
Identify patterns, relationships, and possible drivers.

**3️⃣ What are the key insights?**
Convert analysis into meaningful findings.

**4️⃣ What could be improved?**
Identify opportunities and potential problems.

**5️⃣ What should we do next?**
Provide practical, data-driven recommendations.
